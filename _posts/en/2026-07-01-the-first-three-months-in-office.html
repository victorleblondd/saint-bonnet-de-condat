A term of office focused on action

Since taking office on 22 March 2026, we have wanted to act quickly to meet the municipality's needs and improve residents' quality of life. These first three months have been particularly intense, full of work but also of discoveries.

As the weeks went by, we found several pending files and equipment failures that had been known about for a long time. We wanted to bring concrete solutions quickly and to repair the equipment essential to the smooth running of the municipality.

Much work has been started, in particular on the water springs, where some interventions had become urgent. Specialist companies stepped in to put the chlorination pumps back into service and to work on the lifting pumps of the sewerage network.

Cleaning the cemetery was also one of the priorities. Thanks to several councillors and volunteers, this place of remembrance is now cleaner and more welcoming.

A big thank you to everyone who helped put flowers around the municipality, and to all the residents who, through their commitment, their availability or their help, bring Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat to life.

The main actions undertaken

  • Upkeep of the village and flowers
  • Improvement and cleaning of the hamlets
  • Work on the water and sewerage networks
  • Roll-out of fibre optic broadband
  • Adjustment of the street lighting, pending its modernisation
  • Support for associations
  • Launch of the “Raconte-moi Saint-Bonnet” project

The next priorities

Many projects remain to be carried out. The priorities include the upkeep of the municipal roads, some of which are deteriorating, and the trimming of vegetation along the municipal roads, which has become essential in several areas for the safety of road users.

A committed municipal team

These first months were also an opportunity to meet many residents and to talk with State services, local authorities, our institutional partners and the people who make our area work.

Our commitment is to continue this work in a spirit of closeness, transparency and dialogue, involving residents in the life of the municipality, so that together we can prepare the future of Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat.

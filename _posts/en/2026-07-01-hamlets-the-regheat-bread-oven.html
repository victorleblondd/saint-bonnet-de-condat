As part of the programme to maintain and enhance the municipal heritage, a major clean-up was carried out in the village of Réghéat.

The area around the communal bread oven was completely cleared and cleaned to make this emblematic feature of the local heritage fully visible again. The access routes were also cleared and made safe. This work reflects the municipal team's wish to improve the quality of life in all the villages and hamlets of the municipality.

More upkeep throughout the summer

The municipal teams are continuing:

  • mowing the verges;
  • maintaining the paths;
  • cleaning the public spaces, including the playground;
  • gradually restoring the various hamlets.

The aim: a welcoming, clean and pleasant municipality, for residents and visitors alike.

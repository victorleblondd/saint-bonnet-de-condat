To commemorate 8 May 1945, the municipality of Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat gathered at the war memorial to pay tribute to those who fought for our freedom.

We were honoured to welcome Major Vermande, who attended our ceremony. The wreath was laid by Raphaël. Secondary school pupils were also present, showing how important it is to pass on the duty of remembrance to younger generations.

The municipality warmly thanks the many residents who came to share this moment of reflection. The ceremony ended with drinks at the Bar de la Santoire.

“To remember is to pass on the values of peace, freedom and solidarity on which our Republic is founded.”

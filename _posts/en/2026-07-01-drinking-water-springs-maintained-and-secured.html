As soon as we took office, bringing the drinking water installations up to standard and maintaining them was treated as a priority. Several springs supply the municipality.

The municipal springs underwent a major cleaning and clearing operation, carried out by the technical staff in the first weeks of the term, to ensure that they are accessible, safe and working properly. Protecting the water resource is a major issue for our municipality and for future generations.

Our first actions

  • Complete cleaning around the springs
  • Clearing of the protection areas
  • Repair of the technical access routes
  • Inspection of the existing installations
  • Monitoring of the quality of the water supplied
  • Preparation of future regulatory works

Responsible management

Protecting the resource for the long term, securing the municipal equipment, anticipating droughts, gradually improving the infrastructure and complying with health standards: guaranteeing good-quality water for all is an essential responsibility for our municipality.

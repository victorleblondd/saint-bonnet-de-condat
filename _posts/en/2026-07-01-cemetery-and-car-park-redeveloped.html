To improve access to the cemetery and offer a more pleasant setting for families and visitors, the municipality has undertaken major cleaning and redevelopment work on the municipal car park and its surroundings.

The car park was in poor condition, with many piles of materials that greatly reduced the parking space and spoiled the look of the site.

The work carried out

  • Complete cleaning of the car park and the area around the cemetery
  • Removal of materials and rubble that had built up over several years
  • Levelling and reshaping of the ground, for easier and safer parking
  • Reorganisation of the waste collection point
  • Upkeep of the surrounding area

Better access for everyone

Access to the cemetery is easier for families and visitors, parking is simpler, especially during ceremonies, and the site, levelled and cleared, is safer. Regular upkeep will continue to keep the surroundings clean and welcoming, with respect for this place of remembrance.

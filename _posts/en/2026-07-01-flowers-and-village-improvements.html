Since the start of the term, the municipality has taken several steps to improve the living environment and the image of the municipality. Many areas have been cleaned, maintained and improved thanks to the work of the municipal staff, the councillors and several volunteer residents.

Actions carried out in 2026

  • Installation and upkeep of planters
  • Planting of flowers in the village centre, especially at the town hall roundabout
  • Cleaning of public spaces
  • Upkeep of the municipal flower beds
  • Enhancement of the entrances to the village
  • Residents taking part in the flower displays

Thank you to residents

The municipality warmly thanks everyone who puts flowers out and looks after their property. Thanks to these initiatives, the village keeps its charm and appeal. Every little gesture helps to make our surroundings more beautiful.

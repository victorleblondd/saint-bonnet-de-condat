In Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat, dialogue and cooperation with elected representatives and institutional partners are essential to move the municipality's projects forward.

On 23 May 2026, we were honoured to welcome Mr Bernard Delcros, senator of the Cantal. On 1 June 2026, we welcomed Mr Stéphane Sautarel, senator of the Cantal.

These meetings were an opportunity to discuss current and future projects, the needs of our area and the issues we deal with every day. The senators praised the energy of the municipal team and confirmed their support for our projects.

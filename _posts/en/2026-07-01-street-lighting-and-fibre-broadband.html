Two priorities from the start of the term: adapting the street lighting and preparing for the arrival of fibre optic broadband in the municipality.

New lighting for the municipality

From the first weeks of the term, the municipal team worked on the street lighting. The new times provide:

  • lighting from 6:00 in the morning until daybreak;
  • lighting in the evening until midnight;
  • lighting in the Les Rochers area until midnight in summer and during the school holidays;
  • better safety for residents and road users.

The upkeep of the lights, the switch to LEDs and bringing the installations up to standard are among the actions under way.

Fibre is coming to Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat

Fibre optic broadband is being rolled out in the municipality. Poles are being installed and cables laid in several areas, in the village centre and in the hamlets. This long-awaited project will bring very high-speed internet access, better digital coverage and new opportunities for remote working and businesses.

The service is expected to start in autumn 2026.

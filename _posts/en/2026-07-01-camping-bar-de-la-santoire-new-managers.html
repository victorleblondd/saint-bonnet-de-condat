Thank you, Dennis and Jelle

After several years running the Camping-Bar de la Santoire, Dennis and Jelle have decided to hand over. The municipality warmly thanks them for their welcome, their professionalism and their contribution to the appeal of the municipality, and wishes them all the best in their new projects.

Welcome, Kim and Jeroen

The municipality welcomes Kim and Jeroen, the new managers of the Camping-Bar de la Santoire. We wish them every success in this new adventure in Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat.

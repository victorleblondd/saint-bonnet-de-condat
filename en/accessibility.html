The municipality of Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat is committed to making its website accessible, in accordance with Article 47 of French Law no. 2005-102 of 11 February 2005.

This accessibility statement applies to the website saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr, in French, English and Dutch. It is a translation: the French statement (Déclaration d'accessibilité) is the reference.

Compliance status

{%- if a.etat == "totalement conforme" %}

The website saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr is fully compliant with the French accessibility framework (RGAA, référentiel général d'amélioration de l'accessibilité), version 4.1.2.

{%- elsif a.etat == "partiellement conforme" %}

The website saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr is partially compliant with the French accessibility framework (RGAA), version 4.1.2, because of the non-compliances listed below.

{%- else %}

The website saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr is not compliant with the French accessibility framework (RGAA), version 4.1.2.

{%- endif %}

Exemptions for disproportionate burden

None.

Content not subject to the accessibility obligation

  • The walk route sheet in PDF from the Volcans d'Auvergne Regional Nature Park, linked from the Discover page: third-party content that is neither funded nor developed by the municipality and is not under its control.
  • The hike documents from the Destination Haut Cantal tourist office (route guides and lists of accommodation in PDF, GPX and KML tracks), offered for download on the Tourism page: third-party content that is neither funded nor developed by the municipality and is not under its control. The step-by-step route and the presentation of each hike are given as text on the page (in French).
  • The OpenStreetMap base maps and IGN aerial photos, and the Atmo Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes air quality forecast maps, shown in the maps on the Tourism page: third-party content. The useful information is also given as text: the list of hikes, the weather map table and the municipality's air quality index.

PDF documents

The posters published as PDFs in the News & Alerts section are images, in French. Their full text is given on the page, below each notice, with the “Show the text of the notice” button, in French on the French page and translated on the English and Dutch pages.

Remedies

Use this procedure in the following case: you have reported to the town hall an accessibility problem that prevents you from accessing a piece of content or a service on the website, and you have not received a satisfactory answer.

  • Write a message to the Defender of Rights (Défenseur des droits) (in French);
  • Contact the Defender of Rights' delegate in your region (in French);
  • send a letter by post (free of charge, no stamp needed): Défenseur des droits, Libre réponse 71120, 75342 Paris CEDEX 07.

Personal data

This website has no forms that send data, no cookies and no audience measurement tools. The host may record technical connection data, such as the IP address, to keep the service secure.

Language. If you choose a language with the flags at the top of the page (French, English, Dutch), or close the language suggestion, your browser remembers it on your device (local storage), so that the website can offer you that language. This information never leaves your device.

Tourism page. To show the weather, the air quality and the maps, your browser contacts Open-Meteo (forecasts), Atmo Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes (air quality), OpenStreetMap (base maps) and the IGN (aerial photos) directly; like any website you visit, they receive your device's IP address. Only the coordinates of places are sent to Open-Meteo: those of the village, of the municipalities on the weather map, of the start of a hike or of the point you choose on the map. If you use “Show my location”, your position is only used in your browser, to centre the map and sort the hikes: the website does not pass it on to any service; as whenever you move a map, the images of the area shown are requested from the base map service. The hike filters and search work in your browser.

Notifications. If you turn on notifications, the website stores the subscription address provided by your device and its encryption keys: no name, no phone number, no email address. They are only used to send you the town hall's notices, are hosted in the European Union (Supabase, Paris) and are deleted as soon as you turn off notifications or they are no longer valid. The language of your notifications is stored only on your device. Notifications go through your device's notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla).

If you have any questions, contact the town hall.

Personal data

This website has no forms, no cookies and no audience measurement tools. The host may record technical connection data, such as the IP address, to keep the service secure.

Language. If you choose a language with the “Français · English · Nederlands” links, or close the language suggestion, your browser remembers it on your device (local storage), so that the website can offer you that language. This information never leaves your device.

Notifications. If you turn on notifications, the website stores the subscription address provided by your device and its encryption keys: no name, no phone number, no email address. They are only used to send you the town hall's notices, are hosted in the European Union (Supabase, Paris) and are deleted as soon as you turn off notifications or they are no longer valid. The language of your notifications is stored only on your device. Notifications go through your device's notification service (Apple, Google or Mozilla).

If you have any questions, contact the town hall.

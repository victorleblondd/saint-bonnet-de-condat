Town hall services

Post office counter (Point Poste)

The post office counter is in the town hall. It is open on Mondays, Tuesdays, Thursdays and Fridays, from 9:00 to 12:00.

Hiring the village hall

The village hall (salle des fêtes) is available for family events and events organised by associations.

Freefor residents of Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat

€50for people from outside the municipality

Bookings through the town hall: {{ m.telephone }} or {{ m.email | replace: "@", "@" }}.

Bulky waste collection

The municipality collects bulky items on the last Friday of the month. From January 2027, the collection will take place once every two months.

You must register at the town hall before the collection, on {{ m.telephone }}.

Free photocopies

The town hall offers residents a free photocopying service, in black and white only (no colour copies), for their everyday administrative paperwork. Ask at the town hall office.

Used ink cartridges

Drop off your used ink cartridges at the town hall, all year round. They are given to the school in Lugarde, which has them recycled to help pay for the children's outings, equipment and activities.

Cantal library service

Every Friday afternoon, make the most of the library service: thousands of books, magazines, audiobooks and digital resources. Registration and reservations on culture.cantal.fr (in French).

Drinking water and sewerage

Several springs supply the municipality. Maintaining them and the quality of the water supplied are a priority for the municipal team: protecting today the resource of tomorrow.

A water problem?

Leak, water cut, cloudy water: let the town hall know on {{ m.telephone }}.

Laurent Golliard, 1st deputy mayor in charge of water and sewerage: 06 78 33 66 17.

Paying your water bill

Cheques are no longer accepted. You can pay by bank card, by bank transfer or by direct debit.

To pay by direct debit, bring to the town hall your bank details (RIB) in your name, an identity document and, if needed, proof of address.

Farmers: tanks for livestock

Tanks for watering livestock are filled at the old Frayssinet water tower. Please use it with care, so that the next users can use it too.

It is forbidden to fill tanks with water from the municipal network.

Work carried out in 2026

  • Cleaning and clearing of the springs and their protection areas
  • Chlorination pumps back in service
  • Work on the sewerage lifting pumps
  • Monitoring of the quality of the water supplied

Drought: water use restrictions

Under prefectoral order no. 2026-224-DDT of 15 October 2026, the municipality (Rhue management area) is placed on “crisis” level. These measures apply until 31 October 2026 inclusive, unless a new order is issued.

Restrictions may change. To find out which ones apply today and to read the full order (in French), go to vigieau.gouv.fr, the official French government website. The French text of the order is the reference.

Water use restrictions for individuals, “crisis” level, under the prefectoral order of 15 October 2026
UseRule
Watering vegetable gardens (including non-agricultural greenhouses)Forbidden from 8:00 to 20:00.
Watering lawns, flower beds, ornamental gardens and green spacesForbidden.
Watering trees and shrubsForbidden, except plants less than 3 years old: watering allowed from 20:00 to 8:00, on 2 nights a week at most, after requesting authorisation from the DDT (departmental agency) and posting the chosen dates on site. Totally forbidden if there is a shortage of drinking water.
Sports groundsWatering forbidden, except for sports grounds used for national or international events.
Washing vehiclesForbidden for private individuals and for businesses, except where required for health reasons.
Private swimming poolsFilling forbidden. Emptying into the natural environment forbidden.
Swimming pools open to the publicFilling forbidden, except where required for health reasons and approved by the ARS (regional health agency).
Public and private fountainsForbidden, unless they run in a closed circuit.
Cleaning façades, roofs, pavements, roads and sealed surfacesForbidden, except where required for health or safety reasons.
Ponds and lakesFilling forbidden. Emptying into rivers and streams forbidden, except with specific administrative authorisation.
Canyoning and river trekkingForbidden, except under the departmental protocol governing these activities.

Rainwater collected in storage tanks is not affected by these restrictions. Failure to comply with the restrictions is liable to penalties.

Street lighting

Street lighting times have been adjusted:

  • lights on from 6:00 in the morning until daybreak;
  • lights on in the evening until midnight;
  • the Les Rochers area lit until midnight in summer and during the school holidays.

A light is out? Let the town hall know. Nathalie Metayer, 2nd deputy mayor, is in charge of street lighting.

Fibre optic broadband

Fibre optic broadband is being rolled out in the municipality: poles and cables are being installed in several areas, in the village centre and in the hamlets.

The service is expected to start in autumn 2026.

France Services in the Pays Gentiane

At the France Services centre in Riom-ès-Montagnes, a team helps you free of charge with everyday paperwork: health, pensions, taxes, employment, housing, online procedures. Many public bodies (CAF, CPAM, MSA, CARSAT, France Travail, Mission locale…) see people there by appointment.

Drop-in sessions are also held in neighbouring town halls. The monthly schedule is published in the News & Alerts.

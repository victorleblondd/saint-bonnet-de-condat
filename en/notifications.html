On this device

Checking…

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr/en/ in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (on recent versions, it is in the “⋯three dots” menu), then “Add to Home Screen”. If the “Open as Web App” option appears, leave it turned on. Tap “Add”.
  3. Open the website using the new “Saint-Bonnet” icon on your Home Screen.
  4. Tap “Turn on” in the blue banner at the top of the home page (or come back to this page), then “Turn on notifications” and “Allow”.

You need iOS 16.4 or later. On iPhone and iPad, notifications only work from the Home Screen icon.

On Android

  1. Open saint-bonnet-de-condat.fr/en/ in Chrome, tap “Turn on notifications” on this page, then “Allow”.
  2. To have the icon on your Home screen: tap the ⋮three vertical dots menu, then “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”).

What you will receive

A notification for each new notice from the town hall published in News & Alerts: water cuts, works, closures, events. It also appears on the lock screen.

Notifications arrive in the language of the page on which you turned them on: in English here, when a translation is available, otherwise in French.

Your data

The website only stores the technical subscription address provided by your device: no name, no phone number, no email address. It is hosted in the European Union and deleted as soon as you turn off notifications.

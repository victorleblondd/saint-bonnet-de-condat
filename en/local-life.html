Associations

Associations and their volunteers are a real asset for our municipality. Thanks to their commitment, Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat remains a lively, welcoming and dynamic village.

Les 5 Bonnets

Founded in 2026, the association Les 5 Bonnets was set up to liven up local life and to offer events and get-togethers all year round. It organises the summer farmers' market.

Contact: barresisabelle15190@gmail.com

Les Rochers en Folie

Every year, the association organises the village fête on the first weekend of August: an open-air dance and sausage and chips at the refreshment stall.

Hunting society

The hunting society plays an essential role in managing and preserving wildlife and natural areas. Quentin Boutal was elected its president in 2026.

Heritage association

Currently dormant, the Heritage association is looking for volunteers to take over, with the project “Raconte-moi Saint-Bonnet” (Tell me about Saint-Bonnet).

Events of the year

  • 8 May

    Commemoration of 8 May 1945

    Ceremony at the war memorial.

  • Fridays in summer

    Farmers' market

    Producers, craftspeople, residents and visitors come together to share the riches of the local land. In 2026, from 17 July to the end of August, with Les 5 Bonnets.

  • First weekend of August

    Village fête

    Dance and refreshment stall, with Les Rochers en Folie.

  • 11 November

    Commemoration of 11 November 1918

    Ceremony at the war memorial.

Other events are announced in the News & Alerts.

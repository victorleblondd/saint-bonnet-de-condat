Issue no. 1 · summer 2026

A word from the mayor

Dear residents of Saint-Bonnet,

We are pleased to present the first issue of La Gazette des Saint-Bonnetoux, our municipality's new newsletter.

Its aim is to keep you regularly informed about life in Saint-Bonnet-de-Condat, current projects, completed works, upcoming events and the work of the municipal council.

To keep in regular touch with all residents, La Gazette des Saint-Bonnetoux will come out twice a year, with a summer edition and a winter edition.

We hope that this publication will meet your expectations and help to strengthen communication and the vitality of our municipality.

We hope you enjoy reading it.

The Mayor and the Municipal Council
